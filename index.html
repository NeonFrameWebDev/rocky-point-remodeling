WHAT WE DO

Our Services

From quick refreshes to full property overhauls, we handle every phase of the project in Rocky Point.

Kitchen Remodeling

Full kitchen renovations from concept to completion. Custom cabinets, countertops, backsplash, and appliance integration. We transform kitchens that make properties stand out.

Bathroom Renovation

Complete bathroom transformations. Walk-in showers, custom tile surrounds, new vanities, and fixtures. We build bathrooms that actually sell and rent properties.

Flooring

Tile, Saltillo, ceramic, porcelain, laminate, and decorative concrete. We install and finish floors built to handle Rocky Point heat, foot traffic, and rental wear.

Painting & Finishing

Interior and exterior painting, texture repair, and drywall finishing. Clean, professional finishes that protect your investment and give properties that polished look.

Custom Tile & Stonework

Specialty tile installation, decorative stonework, and custom patterns throughout kitchens, baths, and living areas. Built to last in any climate.

Outdoor Living

Patios, pergolas, outdoor kitchens, and palapa-style shade structures designed for the Rocky Point lifestyle. Built for the beach, the sun, and the salt air.

Full Property Renovations

Complete property overhauls managed start to finish. One point of contact, one budget, one timeline. Ideal for investment flips and large vacation rental upgrades.

Commercial Work

Business spaces, rental unit upgrades, and commercial property improvements in Puerto Penasco. We work with property managers, developers, and business owners.
